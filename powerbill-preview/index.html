Here to help

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How is my bill calculated?

Your current reading minus your previous reading gives your consumption. Your bill then shows the applicable tariff, fixed charge and tax as separate lines.

Who adds my meter?

A billing officer assigns your meter and opening reading to your account. Once assigned, you can submit readings and view your bills.

Where can I find my receipt?

Open a paid bill or the Receipts section of your dashboard. You can view the payment reference and print the receipt for your records.

What if a charge looks wrong?

Open Complaints in your dashboard and link your question to the relevant bill. You can follow its status and read the billing team’s response.

Can I create an account on this preview?

This preview lets you explore the design with sample data. Registration and sign-in are not connected to the live database yet. No account is created and no payment is collected here.
